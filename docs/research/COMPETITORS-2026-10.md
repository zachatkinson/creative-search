## Features at least two of them offer that Creative Search 1.0 lacks, most important first

1. **Custom fields and ACF search:** FiboSearch, SearchWP, Relevanssi, Advanced Woo Search, YITH and Algolia have it. Creative Search has a developer filter only.
2. **Add to cart from the suggestions:** Advanced Woo Search, YITH, Premmerce and itthinx.
3. **WPML, Polylang and TranslatePress integration:** FiboSearch, SearchWP, Relevanssi, Advanced Woo Search and YITH.
4. **Filters or facets beside the search:** itthinx, Advanced Woo Search, Doofinder and Algolia. WooCommerce's own filters cover the results page.
5. **Query redirects:** SearchWP, Relevanssi and Doofinder.
6. **Pinning results to a query:** Relevanssi, Doofinder and SearchWP. Creative Search's boosts are global.
7. **Hiding out-of-stock products:** Advanced Woo Search, YITH and Algolia. Creative Search already honours WooCommerce's "Hide out of stock items".
8. **A full-screen or mobile overlay:** YITH, SearchWP and FiboSearch.
9. **Telling the shopper about a correction:** Relevanssi, Advanced Woo Search and Premmerce. Creative Search shows it on the results page only.
10. **Page-builder widgets:** YITH, Advanced Woo Search, itthinx and Premmerce. Creative Search has a block, a shortcode and a widget, but no Elementor widget.
11. **Variations as their own results:** Advanced Woo Search and YITH.
12. **Voice search:** FiboSearch and Doofinder.
13. **Google Analytics events:** FiboSearch and Advanced Woo Search. These are client-side only, if ever, because of the no-external-requests rule.
14. **User and vendor search:** marketplaces only.
15. **PDF and document search:** SearchWP and Relevanssi (Relevanssi through an external service).
16. **Multisite-wide search:** SearchWP, Relevanssi and Algolia.
17. **AI, semantic or visual search:** SaaS only; out of scope (no external services).
