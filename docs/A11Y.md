# Accessibility

Two layers: axe-core runs in CI on every screen and state (Playwright), and once per release a person who did not write the code runs the 15-minute script below with VoiceOver on Safari and NVDA on Firefox, and records the result here.

## Rules (spec: Quality bar → Accessibility)

The search bar is an APG list-autocomplete combobox: a label, `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, `aria-activedescendant`, options with accessible names ("KAZUMI Skate Deck, Dealer, $84.99, in stock"; since 1.3 a row whose name does not show the matched words ends with what did, "…, Matches Design: Fire", read as part of the option's name, D-105); keyboard parity (Down, Up, Enter, Escape, Home, End, Tab, Alt+Down); a polite live region that says how many suggestions; focus never trapped; 24 px targets (44 px rows on desktop, 48 px on a phone); visible focus; `prefers-reduced-motion` honoured; every admin screen keyboard-complete; colour never the only signal.

## The manual script (15 minutes)

Run on the shop with the block in the header and a catalogue of at least 300 products. Record pass/fail per step, the screen reader's words where they matter, and the date and tester.

### Shop

1. Load the shop's front page. Tab to the search field. Hear: the label, "combobox", "collapsed" (or equivalent).
2. Type `kaz`. Hear the live region: "6 suggestions" (or the count). The panel is visible under the field.
3. Press Down. Hear the first row: product name, price, stock. The row is visually highlighted and its name is written into the field (D-091). Focus stays in the field (the caret is still there). The part of each name you did not type is bold (D-095), and the page behind the panel is dimmed (D-097).
4. Press Down until the last row ("See all results for kaz"), then once more: the field shows `kaz` again with nothing highlighted; Down again highlights the first row.
5. Press Up: the field shows `kaz` with nothing highlighted; Up again: the last row ("See all results for kaz"), with `kaz` still in the field.
5a. Press Escape, then Alt+Down to reopen the panel with nothing highlighted, then Up: the highlight goes straight to the last row.
6. Press Escape: the panel closes, the text stays. Press Escape again: the text clears.
7. Type `kazumi deck`, press Down twice, press Enter: the product page opens.
8. Go back. The field has the last query; press Alt+Down: the panel reopens with the last answer.
9. Type `snowboard`. Hear "No suggestions". The panel shows a no-results row.
10. Clear the field with the clear button (Tab to it, Enter). Hear the field is empty.
11. Type `deck` and press Enter without opening a row: the results page loads. Hear the result count ("23 results for deck") as the page's heading or first announcement.
12. On the results page, Tab to the sort menu: "Relevance" is the selected option; change it to "Price: low to high" with the keyboard; the list reorders.
13. Zoom to 200 %: the panel and the results stay usable, nothing overlaps.
14. Turn on reduced motion in the OS: the panel appears without a fade.
15. On a phone (or a 375 px viewport with touch): tap the field, type, tap a row. Rows are at least 48 px tall. The panel never scrolls (D-092).
15a. With pages switched on, type `size guide`: the last rows are under a "Pages" heading, and each is heard as "Size guide, Page" (D-093). On the results page for a query no product matches, the matching page leads, then the help link (D-096).
15b. On a page with the block in button mode (D-099): Tab to the search button; hear "Search, button" and that it opens a dialog. Press Enter: hear the dialog and the field; type without moving focus. Escape closes the panel, a second Escape closes the dialog, and focus is back on the button. Open it again and press the browser's Back: the dialog closes and the page stays. On a phone the dialog fills the screen and the keyboard opens with it.
15c. Search a word others have searched before (`kaz`, with the log seeded): the first rows are suggested searches, each heard as "kazumi deck, Suggested search"; Down writes it into the field and Enter searches it (D-112). Rows such as "kazumi in Decks" are heard with their category and open the search kept to that category (D-113).
15d. Type `mumber fur fire`: the Mumber-Fur row shows the Fire design's photo, and its name is heard with ", Matches Design: Fire" (D-104, D-105). Type a SKU (`ORB-CLA-00007`): hear ", Matches SKU ORB-CLA-00007".
15e. With "Add to cart from the panel" on (Tools → Suggestions): Down to a simple product; hear "Shift+Enter adds it to the cart." after its name. Press Shift+Enter: hear "Orbit Wheels, Classic added to your cart." and the mini-cart updates; focus stays in the field. The "Add" a pointer sees is not reached by Tab (D-115).
15f. Type `kazumi dealr`: hear "Showing results for “kazumi dealer”" in the panel; on the results page the same line, without a "search instead" link (D-114).
15g. Open a category (Decks) and Tab into its search form: hear "Only in Decks, checkbox, checked". Search `kazumi`: the results page reads "3 results for “kazumi” in Decks" and its link "Search all products for “kazumi”." returns the whole shop. Untick the box and search again: the whole shop (D-117).

### Admin (keyboard only)

16. WooCommerce → Search → Analytics: Tab through the period selector, the chart (it has a text alternative), the tables (column and row headers are announced), the Explain and Export buttons.
17. Synonyms: add a synonym with the keyboard alone; hear the notice; delete it; hear the notice.
18. Boosts and exclusions: add a rule and a fallback product by SKU; hear the notice.
19. Tools: start a rebuild; the progress is announced (live region); Cancel is reachable. The Suggestions section's four switches and the custom fields' checkboxes and text box are labelled and in the tab order.
19a. Redirects and pins: add a redirect (words and a page address) and a pin (words and a SKU) with the keyboard alone; hear each notice; remove each with its checkbox and the Remove button; hear the notice (D-111).
20. Product edit screen: the Search panel's boost and exclude fields are labelled and in the tab order.

## v0.6.0 (the block and the results page)

**Automated:** axe-core (WCAG 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2 A and AA tags; serious and critical fail the build) on the block closed, open, with no suggestions and after an error, on Storefront's, Kadence's and Twenty Twenty-Five's results pages and their no-results state; chromium, Firefox and WebKit at desktop and phone widths: 85 runs green. The APG script steps 1 to 8 and 11 to 12 are automated in `tests/e2e/search-block.spec.ts` and `results.spec.ts` (the `aria-*` state after each key, the live region's words, focus kept in the field, Enter, Escape twice, Tab, Alt+Down, the results page's Relevance sort). One axe finding fixed on the way: options named by `aria-label` did not match their visible text (WCAG 2.5.3); they are now named by their text with visually hidden separators (D-064).

**Manual:** not run. The script above needs VoiceOver and NVDA and a person who did not write the code; it is milestone 8's (spec: "the manual accessibility pass").

## v0.7.0 (the admin)

**Automated:** axe-core, the same tags and threshold, on each of the four tabs of WooCommerce → Search, on the Synonyms tab with a row and a notice, on the Tools tab with a test search's explanation, and on the product editor's Search panel; chromium, Firefox and WebKit at desktop and phone widths: 40 runs green (`tests/e2e/admin.spec.ts`). Script steps 16, 17, 19 (the test search) and 20 are automated by keyboard alone: the focus is reached with Tab (Option+Tab on WebKit, as Safari's default skips links), a synonym is added, tested and deleted, a test search is run, an Analytics period is chosen by type-ahead, and the product's Search tab is opened with Enter. Every tab's visible inputs have ids for their labels and unique ids (an integration test renders each tab). One finding on the way: WooCommerce's product picker (selectWoo) fails axe (an unlabelled search field, `aria-expanded` on a role that does not allow it), so fallback products are chosen in a labelled SKU field (D-069).

**Manual:** not run; milestone 8's, with VoiceOver and NVDA.


## v1.0.0 (languages)

**Automated:** the same axe tags and threshold on the French shop and admin (`tests/e2e/french.spec.ts`), on Chromium, Firefox and WebKit at desktop and phone widths: the open suggestion panel in French with French prices, the French results page, its no-results state, and WooCommerce → Search in French. The French accessible names were checked: the French product name, the price as "84,99 $" and "En stock" for a product row, the live region's "N suggestions", and "Voir les N résultats pour …". `<html lang="fr-CA">` is set by WordPress from the locale, so screen readers switch voice.

**Found and fixed in this pass:**
- Up from the field with no suggestion chosen went to the second-to-last row. It now goes to the last, as the APG pattern expects; step 5a of the script now checks it.
- The panel's last row said "See all 1 results" (in French, "Voir les 1 résultats"). It now has a singular (D-077).
- A parent category was announced as "(0)" products (D-078).
- When nine rows overflow the panel, axe reports `scrollable-region-focusable`. In this pattern the options are reached by `aria-activedescendant` and scrolled into view by the arrow keys, which the test proves. That one rule is exempt for the open panel only (D-079; withdrawn in 1.1, where the panel no longer scrolls, D-092).

**Manual:** **not run.** The 20-step script above needs VoiceOver on Safari and NVDA on Firefox, run by a person who did not write the code. It is the one part of milestone 8 that automation cannot do, and it needs Zach or a tester Zach chooses. When it is done, add a row to the record table with the date, the tester and the words heard, and file any failure as a bug against 1.0.x.

## v1.1.0 (the Skatehouse handoff and the Baymard fixes)

**Automated:** the same axe tags and threshold, with no exemption left: D-079's is gone because the panel no longer scrolls (D-092), and the French panel is checked with every rule. New checks in `tests/e2e/handoff.spec.ts`: the open panel with the backdrop and the marked completions; a page in the panel under its group heading; the results page leading with a page and with the help link; the button-mode dialog open, with the panel inside it. Automated by keyboard: the field written as Down and Up move (D-091, with the APG script in `search-block.spec.ts` rewritten to it), the dialog opened by click and by Enter, typing with no tap, Escape twice, Back, the close button, focus returned to the button, and a search from the dialog; with scripts blocked, the button-mode form shows in place and searches. The panel is checked to fit with no scrollbar at 1280×720 and 390×664.

**Found and fixed in this pass:**
- When the panel was trimmed to fit the screen, the field could keep `aria-activedescendant` pointing at a row that had just been removed (axe, `aria-valid-attr-value`, in the French run). A chosen row that is dropped now sends the choice back to the field; a kept one keeps it under its new id (D-092).
- The panel was measured before its rows rendered and before the theme's web font arrived, so it could end 1.5 px below the screen. It is now measured once the rows are on the page, and again when its size changes (D-092).

**Manual:** **not run**, as for 1.0.0: steps 1 to 15b with VoiceOver on Safari and NVDA on Firefox, by a person who did not write the code.

## v1.2.0 (sizes and categories in the query)

**Automated:** `tests/e2e/routing.spec.ts` runs axe on the category page a query opened, with its line, and on the filtered results, on a block theme and a classic one. The line is a paragraph of plain text and one link whose text says where it goes ("Search all products for “wheels”."); it is not a live region, because it is on the page as it loads. The "Categories:" line is a paragraph of links. The search field on the page landed on keeps the query as typed, so a screen-reader user hears what they searched. The redirect is an ordinary page load: focus starts at the top of the new page, as for any search.

**Manual:** **not run**, as for 1.1.0. Add to the script: search "wheels" and confirm the category page's line is read after the heading, and that its link returns to the results.

## v1.3.0 (the rest of docs/research)

**Automated:** `tests/e2e/panel.spec.ts` runs axe on the panel with suggested searches, category scopes, a typo's line and the add-to-cart hint, on a block theme and a classic one; `matches.spec.ts` on a variation's row and card with its "Matches" line; `scope.spec.ts` on the category form with its checkbox and the scoped results; `rules.spec.ts` and the admin loop on the Redirects and pins tab, in English and French. The new rows are options like the others, with no focusable child (APG listbox); the add-to-cart control a pointer sees is `aria-hidden`, its keyboard way (Shift+Enter) is described through `aria-describedby`, and its result goes to the existing live region. The "Matches" line is part of the option's accessible name, so it is heard with the row.

**Manual:** **not run**, as for 1.2.0. Steps 15c to 15g and 19a cover the new controls.
